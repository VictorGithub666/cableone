Our Story

Bringing Entertainment and Connectivity to Kenya

CableOne Ltd is a limited liability company incorporated in Kenya in 2005 under Certificate of Incorporation with the principal objectives of providing digital television services. The company is licensed to operate cable television services and Internet Service Provider in Kenya by Communication Authority of Kenya (CAK).

The company is providing more than 100 HD quality Cable TV channels and high speed broadband internet services across the nation bringing non-stop entertainment and information to viewers.

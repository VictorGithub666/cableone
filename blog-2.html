TV + WiFi Combo: Why Bundling Beats Buying Separately

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Combo Bundling
July 12, 2026 4 min read Combo

Most households don't set out to have two separate accounts for TV and internet, it just happens gradually. You sign up for WiFi first, then a few months later a TV subscription gets added on with a different provider, and suddenly there are two bills, two due dates, and two customer care numbers to call when something goes wrong.

Combo packages exist to undo exactly that. Here's what actually changes when you bundle your TV and WiFi with CableOne, and why the combined price tends to work out lower than paying for each separately.

The convenience of one bill

Every CableOne combo package bundles a full TV subscription with a WiFi tier into a single monthly payment. That means one due date to remember, one account to manage, and one number to call if either your internet or your set-top box needs attention, instead of juggling two providers with two different support lines.

The savings actually add up

Buying a TV subscription and a WiFi package separately means paying full price for each. Combo packages are priced as a bundle, which brings the combined monthly cost down below what the two would cost on their own, without reducing what's included in either service.

  • Full TV Subscription: 200+ channels, full HD picture, Dolby sound
  • A WiFi tier of your choice, from Bronze right up to Platinum speeds
  • Free installation for both, handled in a single technician visit
A combo package isn't a smaller version of TV and WiFi, it's the same two services, priced and delivered together.

One technician visit, not two

Separate providers usually mean separate installation appointments, separate technicians, and separate waiting windows. With a CableOne combo, both the WiFi router and the TV set-top box are set up in the same visit, free of charge, and usually done well within the day.

Who combo packages make the most sense for

If your household already has both a TV and a reasonable internet connection on your radar, families who stream in the evenings, homes with more than one screen in regular use, or anyone tired of receiving two separate invoices, a combo package is almost always the simpler, cheaper route to the same outcome.

Four combo tiers to choose from

CableOne combos pair the full TV subscription with each of the four WiFi speeds, so a lighter household and a busy, device-heavy one can both find a combo that fits, from the entry-level TV + 50Mbps bundle up to TV + 500Mbps for homes that need it all at once.

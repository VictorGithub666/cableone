The Work-From-Home Survival Guide: Choosing Your Ideal WiFi Tier

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. WFH Survival Guide
July 24, 2026 5 min read WiFi

Working from home runs on more than a laptop and a good chair, it runs on a connection that doesn't quit halfway through a client call. And unlike an office, where IT handles the network, a home setup has to hold up against everything else happening in the house at the same time: a partner streaming, kids on a tablet, a smart TV quietly updating in the background.

Here's a practical way to think through which CableOne WiFi tier actually fits your work-from-home setup, not just your budget.

Start with how you actually work

Not every remote job leans on the internet the same way. Someone answering emails and joining the occasional call needs far less than someone in back-to-back video meetings, uploading large files, or working across multiple cloud tools at once.

  • Light use: email, messaging, occasional video calls, Bronze or Silver is usually enough
  • Regular video calls & cloud tools: daily meetings, file sharing, dashboards, Gold is a comfortable fit
  • Heavy, simultaneous use: multiple calls, large uploads, a household streaming at the same time, Platinum keeps things smooth

Account for the rest of the household

Your work calls don't happen in isolation. If your household also streams shows in the evening, has kids doing schoolwork online, or runs smart home devices in the background, all of that shares the same connection your job depends on. It's worth sizing your WiFi tier around the busiest hour of your day, not the quietest one.

The right WiFi tier isn't the cheapest one that "should" work, it's the one that holds up during your household's busiest hour.

Why unlimited data matters more than people realise

Remote work quietly uses more data than most people expect, video calls running for hours, large files syncing to the cloud, screen shares, recorded meetings. Every CableOne WiFi package includes unlimited data on every tier, so a demanding work day never runs into a data cap partway through.

A simple test before you upgrade

If your video calls freeze during peak hours, if uploads crawl while someone else is streaming, or if you find yourself asking others in the house to "get off the WiFi" during an important meeting, that's a clear sign your current tier is undersized for how your home actually uses the internet during the day.

Free installation, no long commitment to test it out

Every CableOne WiFi package comes with free installation, so trying a higher tier to see whether it solves the problem doesn't come with extra setup costs. And if it turns out you need more, or less, you can move up or down a tier as your work setup changes.

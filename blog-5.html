Why CableOne TV Is Nairobi's Favourite for Family Entertainment

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. CableOne TV
July 29, 2026 4 min read TV

There's a reason the television is still the one screen a whole family gathers around, news in the morning, cartoons after school, a match on Saturday, a movie on Sunday night. CableOne's TV subscription is built around exactly that: one set-top box, one monthly plan, and a channel guide with something for everyone in the house.

200+ channels, one guide

CableOne TV brings together news, sport, movies, kids' programming and local stations in a single channel guide, all for KShs 2,400 a month. No separate subscriptions for sport, no separate app for kids' shows, it's all accessible from the same remote.

Full HD picture, Dolby sound

Picture quality is where a lot of budget TV packages quietly cut corners. CableOne TV runs in full HD with Dolby sound, so a football match or a movie night actually looks and sounds the way it's meant to, sharper images, richer audio, no compromise for a lower price.

A subscription is only as good as what you actually see and hear on the screen, full HD and Dolby sound aren't extras, they're the standard.

Set up once, done for good

Every CableOne TV subscription includes a set-top box that's installed by a technician at no extra cost. There's no complicated setup to figure out on your own, the box is installed, tuned, and ready to watch, usually within the same visit.

Built for the whole household

A single channel guide covering news, sport, movies, kids' programming and local stations means CableOne TV isn't tuned for one type of viewer, it's built so every member of the household finds something on, whether that's cartoons at 4pm or a match at 8pm.

Pairs well with home WiFi

Households that also want fast home internet alongside their TV subscription can bundle both into a Combo Package at a lower combined monthly price, one bill, one technician visit, and both services live from day one.
